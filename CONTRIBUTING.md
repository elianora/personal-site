# Contribution Guidelines

We appreciate your time investment in contributing to this software. Before you begin, ensure that you understand the sections below. If anything is unclear, reach out to [developer@elianorarose.com](mailto:developer@elianorarose.com) with any requests for clarification.

## Artificial Intelligence Contribution Policy

This repository expressly forbids contributions of any kind by large language models and/or other forms of generative artificial intelligence. Attempts to circumvent this rule will be met with a permanent ban with no warnings.

## Ethos

This software is made with the core ethos outlined below:
- Software is made by humans, for humans
- When using our collective skills, critical thinking, and experiences while creating this software, we measure value by connection and creation, not productivity or financial gain
- We respect consent, thus we will not use any generative pre-trained transformer, large language model, or other form of unauditable system that we cannot guarantee acquired express permission during its data collection and training
- We grow together by working together, thus we will not allow any transformer model or other form of artificial neural network to deprive us of the value and insight made possible by going through the collective creation process when working on this software
- Our well-being outweighs our production in importance, thus we avoid using anthropomorphized systems with unclear and inconclusive research into their mental health effects to guarantee no adverse effects can arise for users of or contributors to this software
- We work on this software in this manner because we are against the industry proliferating these technologies irresponsibly without safeguards in a way that is destructive to many things outside of the technology world without research into and verification of their immediate and downstream effects

This ethos is explicitly **pro-human**. This is not a rejection of technological advancements, nor is it an assertion that there are no valid use cases for generative artificial intelligence tools in a more ideal world. It is, however, in service of ensuring the best outcome for the users of and contributors to this software and the current state of the world necessitates a firm stance on this issue.
